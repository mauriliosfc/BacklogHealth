How to find your organization?
  1. Go to dev.azure.com in your browser
  2. The org name appears right after the first slash in the URL
  3. Example: dev.azure.com/my-company

How to generate a PAT?
  1. Go to dev.azure.com and sign in
  2. Click your profile icon (top right)
  3. Select Personal Access Tokens
  4. Create a token with Work Items (Read) and Project and Team (Read) permissions
  5. Copy and paste the token above
